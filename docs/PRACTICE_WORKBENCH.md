# Practitioner workbench: record first, authority second

**18 October 2026.** This is the implementation plan for advocate workflows built on LawBuddy's statute and case indexes. The workbench now supports police information, anticipatory bail, regular bail, civil adjournment and a research-based legal opinion. It produces a local working draft, fact gaps, forum checks, an issue grid and review prompts. Criminal workflows also show statute and judgment leads; the opinion requires counsel-selected authorities. It does not decide whether relief is available or create a filing-ready document. See the [drafting architecture and instrument taxonomy](DRAFTING_ARCHITECTURE.md).

## Working advocate workflow

1. **Intake.** Record the client's instructions separately from what the FIR, complaint, remand paper, order and annexures actually say. Capture event date, procedural stage, court, sections alleged, adverse facts and desired relief. Every fact should carry a record/page or an "instruction only" marker before it becomes an assertive pleading sentence.
2. **Law gate.** Resolve the exact provision and subsection for the relevant date, jurisdiction and statute. Inspect commencement, notifications, repeal/savings and special-act restrictions. [BNSS sections 173, 480, 482, 483 and 531](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/20099?view_type=browse) anchor the initial criminal workflows. The distinction between an incident before July 2024 and a later procedural step must remain visible, not be collapsed into a section-number swap.
3. **Research gate.** Search primary judgments, match citation identity, inspect the leading opinion and exact paragraph, record the point of law **and the limits of the holding**, then check later treatment. A case list or headnote is a lead, not an argument. Pre-arrest bail is fact-sensitive; the [Supreme Court's discussion of nature, gravity, role and investigation](https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2023/108/108_2023_9_1501_43584_Judgement_17-Apr-2023.pdf) supports a structured issue checklist, not an automatic grant prediction.
4. **Draft and oppose.** Generate only from supplied facts, preserve unknowns as brackets, state the other side's case fairly, and put the strongest contrary fact and a record-backed answer next to each proposed ground. Produce an annexure/page map and an explicit list of missing evidence. For an FIR workflow, create *information for police / a request to record*; the police record and number must never be fabricated.
5. **Hearing and filing.** Prepare a one-page opening, three key points, likely bench questions and fallback relief. Check service, affidavit, format, pagination and the current local rules. Advocate signs off on the law, facts and final document; the official [e-filing platform](https://filing.ecourts.gov.in/pdedev/?p=otherPages%2FAboutUs) handles submission.

## Automation architecture

The existing web workbench deliberately keeps client facts in the browser. It calls the server with statute-section, provision and citation queries only; saving to IndexedDB and JSON export are explicit. The local draft appears immediately while research loads; changing intake facts hides the stale packet and cancels pending research. It can be used for a first draft with no model call. This is a conservative starting point for confidential matters, **not** a promise of encrypted chamber storage or cross-device backup. Users can delete a saved browser matter.

The **Download Word** action creates an editable `.docx` entirely in the browser from the currently prepared packet. The proposed text and a clearly marked internal review page contain the supplied facts, bracketed unknowns, gaps and forum checks. Export is disabled after any intake change and when a known statutory or forum blocker remains. No judgment lead is silently inserted as a legal proposition. The file itself is a client record and should be handled under the chamber's retention and access policy. An advocate can upload the downloaded Word file to Drive if their chamber uses it; direct Drive upload is not connected and this workflow makes no claim about Drive permissions or storage.

For a planned filing date before 1 July 2024, the workbench searches the relevant CrPC section leads and says that dated CrPC text is absent from LawBuddy's versioned statute library. For later filings concerning earlier events, it flags repeal and savings for review. The judgment query has a decision-date cutoff; later-treatment labels still describe the current index and the page discloses its latest indexed decision date. This prevents a later judgment from being presented as authority available on an earlier filing date, while making the remaining historical-law coverage gap visible.

The next data model should represent `matter → event → source document → page/span → assertion → disputed issue → legal proposition → judgment paragraph → draft sentence → review decision`. Each draft sentence needs a trace to a client instruction or document, its verification state, editor and timestamp. Each legal proposition needs a primary source, paragraph, opinion, treatment and applicable date. Retrieval ranks possible sources; it must not silently convert retrieval relevance into legal validity.

Build the service as independent gates: **document ingestion and OCR quality → source/version ledger → event chronology → authority resolution → constrained draft generation → contradiction and omission audit → human review → export**. An OCR failure, missing source, disputed jurisdiction or unknown local rule returns a review item. Store source hash and retrieval time. New official notifications create a reviewable event rather than rewriting old answers. Use role-based matter permissions and audit logs before introducing server-side client-file storage. Consent, retention and deletion controls must precede any hosted multi-user chamber mode.

## Evaluation and launch

Recruit practicing advocates across trial courts and High Courts, including criminal defense and complainant-side work. Use consented, redacted historical files with known orders. A legal editor marks ground truth for fact provenance, missing adverse facts, section applicability, citation identity, proposition support and filing defects. Track false-green statutory results and unsupported legal propositions as safety metrics; track time saved only after quality gates pass. Segment results by court, language, special statute, document quality and incident date. Do not train on private matter files by default.

Ship the first pilot for **research and preparation**, with visible library coverage and source links. Expand to filing preflight and shared matter management only after the source ledger, permissions and practitioner evaluation are strong enough for that use.
