# Legal reports: current implementation

Updated 25 October 2026. The initial MVP was superseded after the named-case report audit. See [Reports repair and submission write-up](REPORTS_REPAIR.md) for the current pipeline, configuration, tests and limitations.

The deliverable contains legal prose and numbered references only. Identity status, source quotations, missing material, model settings, hashes and output-gate results are displayed in a separate review panel. Unresolved or failed results cannot be exported as clean submission documents.

Enter a case name or citation to automatically locate and download a judgment from the Supreme Court collection. Multiple matches offer a case choice. Supplying a numbered judgment remains optional. Case facts, holdings and reasoning are generated from literal quote cards, followed by independent verification calls and configured majority voting. PDF retrieval uses registered sources; arbitrary web links and general High Court search are not supported.
