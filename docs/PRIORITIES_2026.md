# LawBuddy priorities for 2026

**Decision, 15 October 2026:** Make **Bare Act Check** the first reliable product. The next workflow is an advocate's audit of statutory references in a draft. Matter and filing tools should build on that verified statute layer. This complements the existing voice tutor and case-law work in [IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md); it does not replace them.

## Why this order

India Code is a useful official repository of Acts, sections and subordinate legislation, but its [disclaimer](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/disclaimer.jsp?abv=DD&col=123456789%2F2492) says State/UT updating is continuous and does not warrant that material is current. The [Gazette of India](https://egazette.gov.in/) publishes Acts and notifications. A correct check must reconcile consolidated text with the official event that changed or commenced it, then show the evidence and date used.

An Act-level commencement flag cannot safely answer a provision-level question. India Code lists an enforcement date of 1 July 2024 for the [Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS)](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/handle/123456789/20062?col=123456789%2F1362&view_type=search), while [MHA notification S.O. 850(E)](https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/BhartiyaNyayaSanhita_24022024.pdf) expressly excepts section 106(2). The [Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_45_0_00003_2023-22_1763464807080&filename=dpdprules2025.pdf&type=rule) use several commencement intervals; [MeitY also lists a corrigendum and a separate Act enforcement timeline](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa?pageTitle=Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Rules-2025%C3%AF%C2%BF%C2%BC). India Code's [CPC text includes State amendments](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/indiacode/bitstream/123456789/2191/1/aA1908-05.pdf). These are test fixtures, not edge cases to hide.

**Transition is a separate question from the text in force on a date.** [BNS section 358](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20062/1/a2023-45.pdf), [BNSS section 531](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20335/1/a2023-46.pdf), [BSA section 170](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/20063/1/a2023-47.pdf), and [General Clauses Act section 6](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/15374/1/the_general_clauses_act%2C_1897.pdf) contain different savings rules. The [Supreme Court discussed pending CrPC proceedings under BNSS section 531 in a 2026 judgment](https://www.sci.gov.in/sci-get-pdf/?diary_no=444652025&from=latest_judgements_order&order_date=2026-05-19&type=j). Therefore, the app must not declare that every matter after 1 July 2024 uses only the new criminal codes, or that an IPC-to-BNS section mapping determines the applicable law. It should ask for the relevant event and proceeding dates, show the savings provision, and flag applicability for lawyer review.

## Phased roadmap

| Priority | User problem and deliverable | Implementation boundary and release gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P0 · Bare Act reliability** | A student or advocate checks a section or uploaded copy and sees the official text, version date, jurisdiction, differences and source PDF. | Create canonical Act/provision IDs; preserve every official source snapshot with URL, retrieval time, SHA-256 and page/section spans; model amendment, repeal, corrigendum and *provision-level* commencement events. Resolve `provision + jurisdiction + as-of date`, with explicit **verified text / changed / not commenced / source conflict / unknown** results. No green result on missing or conflicting sources. Release on the BNS 106(2), DPDP phased rules, CPC State amendment and repeal/savings fixtures above, plus regression tests for changed PDFs and extraction failures. |
| **P1 · Advocate draft audit** | A lawyer uploads or pastes a pleading and receives a review queue of every Act, section and quoted passage. | Extract references with document offsets; resolve aliases and subsections; compare against law on the chosen event/filing date and today; link each finding to an official PDF and page. Show old/new wording and unverified items. Export a source-linked review report. Require human acceptance of proposed replacements: IPC, CrPC and Evidence Act crosswalks may be split, merged or substantively changed. Pilot with advocates using redacted drafts; measure true positives, false positives and missed references before widening access. |
| **P2 · Matter cockpit** | A litigation team sees what must be done before the next hearing. | Join an opt-in CNR/matter record to orders, hearing date, assigned tasks, brief checklist and statutory-change alerts. Start with manual CNR entry or user-provided files; obtain approved data access before automated court sync. Store court-data retrieval time and show staleness. The official [eCourts app already provides free case status, cause lists, My Cases, notes, calendar, orders and several languages](https://services.ecourts.gov.in/App/appfaq.html), so a duplicate tracker is not the product. Its [web flow uses CAPTCHA](https://services.ecourts.gov.in/ecourtindia_v6/); do not build a CAPTCHA-bypassing crawler. The [eCourts portal warns of inaccuracies and update delays](https://ecourts.gov.in/ecourts2.0/), so deadline prompts need confirmation against the court record. |
| **P3 · Filing preflight** | Reduce avoidable filing defects and assembly time. | Version a checklist by court/bench and effective date; validate PDF properties, annexure list, index and page references; produce a reviewable bundle report. The [e-filing user manual](https://ecourts.gov.in/ecourts_home/static/manuals/efiling-User-manual.pdf) documents PDF/scanning constraints, but it is an older general manual. Obtain and test each court's current practice directions before claiming compliance. The product prepares the filing; the advocate approves and files it. |
| **Later · Chamber tools** | Preserve context across a matter and reduce administrative work. | Only after advocate pilots show demand: permissioned client/matter documents, conflict checks, draft history, task assignment, client updates, time and invoice records. Enforce matter-level access and audit logs from the first private-document feature. Treat legal confidentiality and personal-data handling as architecture requirements, not marketing copy; see [BCI professional-conduct statement](https://www.barcouncilofindia.org/info/rules-of-professional-conduct) and [MeitY's DPDP materials](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa?pageTitle=Digital-Personal-Data-Protection-Rules-2025%C3%AF%C2%BF%C2%BC). |

### P0 publishing rule

Use a reviewable pipeline: **discover official document → archive bytes and provenance → extract with page anchors → parse proposed legal events → cross-check consolidated text and gazette → human review of uncertain events → publish a dated snapshot → run regression fixtures**. Store both *legal effective time* and *when LawBuddy learned/verified the source*. A corrected source must create a new snapshot and audit entry; it must not silently rewrite an earlier answer. Search and AI may help locate a provision, but the result status comes from the verified event ledger and extracted source spans. “Verified text” means the cited wording was checked against a source; it is not an automatic opinion on applicability to a client's facts.

### Which users first

The primary paid or professional pilot should be **advocates handling statutory research and drafting**. Keep the current tutor valuable to LLB students and judiciary aspirants by using the same source ledger for section comparisons and historical explanations. Build exam practice as a separate experience: the [CLAT UG 2027 syllabus](https://clat2027.consortiumofnlus.ac.in/clat-2027/ug-syllabus.html) emphasizes passage-based legal reasoning and says prior legal knowledge is not required, while the [CLAT PG syllabus](https://consortiumofnlus.ac.in/clat-2027/pg-syllabus.html) uses extracts from judgments, statutes and regulations. A 2020 [survey of 2,800 advocates across eight High Courts](https://vidhilegalpolicy.in/research/a-survey-of-advocates-practicing-before-the-high-courts/) found low perceived early-career earnings in several courts; use this as a pricing hypothesis to test, not a current national estimate.

## Comparable-market signals, not verified performance claims

Pakistan and Bangladesh retain some common statutory lineage with India, including official versions of the [Pakistan Penal Code, 1860](https://pakistancode.gov.pk/pdffiles/administratoracbdabf2b79c8956d6ea4804fcceb92d.pdf) and [Bangladesh Penal Code, 1860](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-11/section-print-2189.html). Their products are useful workflow references; their legal content cannot be transplanted to India.

| Product's own description | Useful product lesson for LawBuddy |
| --- | --- |
| Pakistan's [Legalise](https://legalise.pk/about/) advertises controlled-corpus drafting, federal/provincial statutes, case search, a stamp-duty calculator and jurisdiction tools. | Keep drafting tied to identifiable sources and local jurisdiction. |
| [Pak Legal Database](https://www.paklegaldatabase.com/about-us/) advertises page-linked source PDFs, court notices/cause lists, a case diary, multilingual research and WhatsApp citation retrieval. | Reduce steps from a question or citation to the underlying document; connect research to a matter. |
| Nigeria's [LawPavilion](https://www.lawpavilion.com/cms) advertises matter management, conflict checks, permissions, private firm knowledge, client portal and billing; its [homepage](https://www.lawpavilion.com/) advertises offline legal research. | A full chamber suite is possible, but it is much broader than the first wedge. Offline access may matter for students and court visits after the source ledger is reliable. |
| Bangladesh's [Digital BLD](https://www.bldlegalized.com/) describes searchable, portable digital law reporting for its legal community. | Institutional provenance and easy access can be as important as generative answers. |

These are **vendor or platform claims about features**, not independent evidence of accuracy, adoption, security or legal authority. Test workflows with Indian advocates before copying them. A generic AI research chat or case tracker is also crowded in India: [Manupatra already advertises Central/State Bare Acts, court rules, forms and AI summaries](https://www.manupatrafast.com/defaults/plan/Core%20Plan.pdf?1=), while eCourts supplies the free case utilities above. LawBuddy's measurable wedge is a dated, jurisdiction-aware check with primary-source proof and an honest unresolved state.
