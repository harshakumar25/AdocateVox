Where the printed text can mislead you

The official consolidated text keeps some provisions that courts struck down, and some amendments were never brought into force. LawBuddy flags both.

Part III · Fundamental Rights

Article 21

Protection of life and personal liberty, the provision most read alongside Articles 14 and 19.

 Struck down, still printed

Article 124A (NJAC)

Still in the official text, but the Supreme Court struck down the 99th Amendment in 2015.

 Never brought into force

Article 22

Changes made by the 44th Amendment in 1978 still wait for a commencement date.

 In force 16 Apr 2026

Article 330A

Reservation of seats for women in the House of the People, under the 106th Amendment.
