Is what you are reading accurate law?

Upload a bare act PDF, or your notes, an article, an answer or a commentary. LawBuddy first works out what the document is, then checks it the way that kind of document must be checked: a bare act section by section against the official Gazette text and the judgments that struck parts of it down; notes and articles claim by claim, with every section, quotation and case citation checked against the official text and the Supreme Court's own record.

Checks copies of the Constitution of India, the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam; recognises copies of the repealed IPC, CrPC and Evidence Act; and fact-checks any write-up that cites them. Your file is read and deleted straight away; LawBuddy keeps only its fingerprint.
